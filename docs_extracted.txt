Target Environment

A privacy-first AI-powered customer support and technical assistance platform for Samsung home appliances, designed to assist customers, service technicians, and support representatives in troubleshooting, product information retrieval, specification lookup, and installation guidance.

Executive Summary

SAGE AI (Search Augmented Generative Engine) is a next-generation, self-correcting Multi- Agent Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system designed to provide intelligent technical support and product assistance for Samsung home appliances. The project addresses the limitations of traditional RAG architectures, which often suffer from inaccurate retrieval, hallucinated responses, poor integration with structured databases, and lack of performance validation.

The proposed system combines advanced retrieval techniques, agentic orchestration, and automated evaluation to create a reliable enterprise-grade AI assistant capable of operating on consumer-grade hardware. The knowledge base consists of Samsung appliance user manuals, troubleshooting guides, and a structured SQLite database containing product specifications, reviews, and category information. By integrating both unstructured and structured data sources, the system can answer a wide range of user queries, from troubleshooting technical issues to comparing product specifications and retrieving detailed appliance information.

Technical Architecture & Functionalities

SAGE AI follows a Multi-Agent Hybrid RAG Architecture that combines advanced document retrieval, structured database querying, local LLM inference, and automated evaluation into a single intelligent system.

Knowledge Processing Layer

Before information can be retrieved, documents are processed through an ingestionpipeline.

Parent-Child Chunking

Instead of storing large manuals directly:

Documents are split into large parent chunks.

Parent chunks are further divided into smaller child chunks.

Child chunks are embedded and indexed.

Parent chunks are returned as context during retrieval. Benefits:

More accurate retrieval.

Better contextual understanding.

Reduced information loss.

Retrieval Layer (Corrective RAG)

This layer is responsible for finding the most relevant information.

Dense Retrieval

Uses:

nomic-embed-text

ChromaDB

Retrieves semantically similar chunks even when wording differs. Example:

User:

"Why is my refrigerator making loud noises?" The system can retrieve sections discussing:

Compressor vibrations

even if the exact words "loud noises" are not present.

Sparse Retrieval

Uses:

BM25 (rank_bm25) Useful for:

Error codes

Model Numbers

Technical Terminology

Example:

User:

"Error E21"

BM25 can directly locate the exact occurrence.

Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF)

Results from Dense Search and BM25 are combined. Benefits:

Higher recall

Better coverage

More robust retrieval

Conclusion:

The proposed SAGE AI (Search Augmented Generative Engine) project presents a comprehensive and enterprise-oriented approach to building an intelligent support system for Samsung home appliances. By combining advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation techniques, multi-agent orchestration, structured database querying, and automated evaluation, the system addresses the major limitations of conventional RAG architectures, including hallucinations, poor retrieval accuracy, and limited interaction with structured data.

Overall, SAGE AI demonstrates how modern AI engineering practices, including hybrid retrieval, agentic workflows, self-correction mechanisms, and continuous performance evaluation—can be integrated into a robust and scalable intelligent assistant. The successful implementation of this project will not only provide a practical solution for Samsung appliance support but will also serve as a valuable case study showcasing the potential of next-generation Multi-Agent RAG systems in real-world enterprise environments.
