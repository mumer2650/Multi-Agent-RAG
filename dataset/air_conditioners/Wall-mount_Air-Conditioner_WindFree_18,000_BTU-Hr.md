### Features

- **AI Fast & Comfort Cooling**
  After Fast Cooling lowers the room temperature quickly, AI analyzes the indoor and outdoor environments to switch to WindFree Cooling according to the user's preference. AI learns consumers' usage patterns and spaces to cool them quickly and coolly.

- **AI Energy Mode**
  By using AI to analyze your usage patterns and external conditions, it prevents sudden stops or increases in the compressor’s frequency, reducing energy use by up to 30%. AI Energy mode can also save energy by switching devices to Energy Saving mode at a pre-set threshold.

- **Comfort Drying**
  Enjoy comfortable dehumidification without feeling cold. It maintains the desired temperature while keeping you in the Humidity Comfort Zone (40% to 60%). It reduces energy use by 30% by only creating sufficient cold air for dehumidifying.

- **WindFree™ Comfort Cooling**
  Stay cool without feeling a cold draft. Advanced air flow structure gently and evenly disperses air through 23,000 micro air holes, creating a Still Air environment with very low air speed.

- **WindFree™ Wearable Good Sleep**
  Links to wearable smart devices like the Galaxy Watch. If they detect that you are asleep, it automatically turns on WindFree Cooling and adjusts the cooling performance for a pleasant sleep.

- **WindFree™ Energy Saving**
  Reduces unnecessary energy consumption as it doesn't need to blow powerful blasts of cold air. Consumes 77% less energy than Fast Cooling mode.

- **WindFree™ Low Noise**
  Gently and uniformly disperses air through thousands of micro air holes, generating less air friction and noise compared to Normal cooling.

- **Fast Cooling**
  The twin compressor works stably at any speed with 3x stronger Neodymium magnets. It cools the air 43% faster and disperses air farther and wider.

- **Energy Saving (Digital Inverter Boost)**
  Maintains desired temperature without frequent on/off cycling, reducing energy use by up to 73%.

- **Fast Heating**
  Quickly distributes warm air across an entire room, reaching the target temperature faster than conventional models.

- **Low Noise**
  Twin compressor creates 20 times less vibration, combined with a Twin Tube Muffler to reduce refrigerant flow noise.

- **10 Year Warranty**
  Inverter compressor is designed for long-lasting performance, backed by a 10-year parts warranty.

- **New Slim Outdoor Units**
  Volume reduced by 20% by utilizing compact core components, which also improves energy efficiency by 20%.

- **R32 Refrigerant**
  Conserves the ozone layer with zero ODP and lower GWP compared to R22 or R410A.

- **SmartThings & AI Features**
  Includes Quick Remote, Map View, Voice Control (Bixby/Alexa/Google), Welcome Cooling & Away Care, Smart Forward updates, SmartThings Home Care, SmartThings Air Care, and SmartThings Pet Care.

- **Air Purification & Maintenance**
  Includes Washable PM1.0 Filter, 4-in-1 PM2.5 Filter, Tri-Care Filter, Copper Anti-bacterial Filter, Freeze Wash, 3-step Auto Clean, Easy Filter, and Easy Open Panel.

- **Durability (DuraFin, Triple Protector Plus)**
  Equipped with DuraFin/DuraFin+ anti-corrosion technology, Triple Protector Plus for power surge protection, and Coated Copper Tubes for 7.5x better corrosion resistance.

- **Convenience Features**
