2. The ecosystem integration on this model is brilliant. I love using Multi Control to jump effortlessly between screens, and being able to sync my Galaxy Watch to check real-time stats on the Workout Tracker while a show is playing is incredibly convenient. 3. Massive display for a very reasonable price compared to the higher-end lineups. Colors are bright and punchy with PurColor, and the One UI Tizen software is incredibly fast and intuitive. The built-in 20W speakers are decent for everyday viewing with Adaptive Sound, but a screen this massive definitely deserves to be paired with a dedicated soundbar. (4 stars, posted 2 months ago)
