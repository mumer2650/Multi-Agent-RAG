# Freestanding Dishwasher Wide LED Display

**Price:** Not listed

### Features

* **Simply & intuitively monitor the status (Wide LED Display)**
  Enjoy a simpler and more intuitive way to clean your dishes with a large and wide LED display that is easy to read. You can quickly check the status, settings, remaining time and cycle progress at a glance, even from a distance. So you can effortlessly monitor and control the washing performance.

* **Easily load and safely wash cutlery (Flexible 3rd Rack)**
  A Flexible 3rd Rack provides a dedicated space at the top for small and light items, such as cutlery and utensils. So items stay safe, but are still cleaned thoroughly by powerful jets of water. Available on the DW60DG560 models.

* **Flexibly fit taller and wider dishes (Easy Height Adjustment)**
  A Flexible Interior Space is height adjustable, so the upper rack can be quickly raised up or down by an extra 5 cm with one touch of a trigger. By creating more space in the lower rack you can fit in various sizes and shapes of dishes, including tall pots, wide platters and oversized plates. Available on the DW60DG550, DW60DG560 models.

* **Easily save time & energy on small load (Half Load)**
  Gone are the days of waiting for the dishwasher to be full. The Half Load setting gives you much greater flexibility as it allows you to fill and clean items in either the upper or lower basket independently. So you can save time and energy by running smaller loads and enjoy the convenience of having clean dishes at anytime.

* **Hygienic rinsing to eliminate bacteria (Hygiene)**
  A Hygiene option can be activated with certain programs to get everything hygienically clean. It extends the final rinse and increases the water temperature to 70˚C, which enables deeper cleaning and kills off bacteria and germs. So your dishes, cutlery and cooking utensils are always safe to use.

* **Save time with 60-minute wash & dry (Express Wash (60 min))**
  A 60 minute Express Wash option gets your dishes clean and dry much faster. It’s ideal for smaller loads of lightly soiled items that don’t need really intensive cleaning, such as cups, cutlery and dinner plates, as it will put them through a complete washing and drying cycle within just 60 min.
